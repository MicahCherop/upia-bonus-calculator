Help & Support

Frequently asked questions, eligibility criteria, and system guidance.

How do I qualify for a Full Bonus?
To unlock your full band multiplier, you must meet or exceed all six key performance indicators (KPIs) for the month:
  • Disbursement Rate: ≥ 98.00%
  • Active Customers: ≥ 95.00%
  • New Customers: ≥ 95.00%
  • Overall OTC Rate: ≥ 91.50%
  • DD+7 Rate: ≥ 94.00%
  • New Customer OTC: ≥ 90.00%
What is the 45% Collection Bonus?
If you fall short on Disbursement, Active Customers, or New Customers, you can still salvage a portion of your bonus. If you successfully meet the three strict collection metrics (OTC, DD+7, and New Customer OTC), you will be awarded the 45% Collection Bonus based on your current band.
Why is my payout showing KSh 0 despite hitting my targets?

This is likely due to the Late Arrival Rule.

To be eligible for any bonus in a given performance cycle, you must have reported to work on or before the 5th day of that month. If the system detects a Date Reported after the 5th, you are automatically disqualified from the Base Bonus and Founder's Bonus for that cycle, regardless of your KPI metrics.

How does the Founder's Bonus work?
The Founder's Collection Upside Bonus is a separate reward evaluated entirely on your DD+7 Rate and your total Actual Disbursed amount. As long as you were not disqualified by the Late Arrival Rule, this bonus is calculated independently of your Full/45% eligibility status.
How are Branch Manager (BM) bonuses calculated differently?
A Branch Manager's base salary and performance bands are determined by the number of Pairs they manage (1, 2, or 3 Pairs). Managing more pairs increases the base salary and scales the customer thresholds required to reach higher bands. You can view the exact thresholds on the Band Guide page.

Still need help?

If you believe there is a discrepancy in your customer count, KPI metrics, or Date Reported, you can chat with Ops directly.
